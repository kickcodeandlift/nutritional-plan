REBUILD 8

GER scale how hard each set is

G74-6 reps in reserve. Feeder sets.
G82-3 reps in reserve.
G91 rep in reserve.
G10Stop at the point where the next rep fails, form still perfect. No forced reps.
G11Go to failure, then a rep or more after form breaks. Muscle round level.
G12Beyond failure: DC rest-pause.
G13Max: widowmaker, 15-20 reps without racking.

1x8-12 G10 = 1 working set, 8-12 reps, finishing at G10. MR = muscle round, 1 set at G11. BO = backoff set. Warm-up and feeders come before and are not counted.

  • Limits
    Do not exceed the prescribed sets
    Do not go past the prescribed failure
  • Warm-up
    2 sets x 15-20 reps before the first exercise of each muscle
  • Feeders
    2-3 sets x 4-8 reps at 70-80% of the intended load, G7
    Before the 1st and 2nd exercise of each muscle
    1 feeder set before the other exercises of that muscle
  • MR (muscle round)
    1 all-out set, G11
    Calves: no GER
  • BO (backoff)
    Cut 20-30% of the top set load
    1 set to failure, 10-15 reps, G10
  • DC rest-pause
    Load for 8 reps
    Go to failure, pause 20s
    Max reps, pause 20s
    Failure again
    When block 1 reaches 10-11 reps, raise the load
  • Widowmaker
    Load for failure at 10-12 reps
    Keep going to 15-20 reps
    Rest as needed between reps, do not rack
  • GER on these methods
    The program intro lists rest-pause G11 and widowmaker G12
    The Week 5 day pages tag them G12 and G13
    Where a day page shows a GER, it is shown here
  • Training partner
    Max 1 forced rep per set, including rest-pauses
  • Rest between sets
    Not specified in the program
  • DC stretch after each muscle
    60-120s under load
    Biceps, triceps, lats hang, chest DB, shoulders on cable, hams with bar or DB and straight spine, quads sissy position
    If you hold over 120s, add load
